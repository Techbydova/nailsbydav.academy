What is included

Each course includes the online learning content made available in the current NailsByDav Academy catalog. The exact learning path, lesson modules, and progress tracking depend on the selected course and the account's current access status.

Access and billing

Course access begins after the relevant enrollment and payment confirmation has been received by the active payment flow. If a subscription is inactive or has expired, access is paused until the account is active again or the payment issue is resolved.

Renewal and cancellation

The student area presently does not include a self-service cancellation dashboard. To cancel or request account support, email nailsbydav@gmail.com or use the Contact page. Cancellation stops future renewals for the active billing arrangement; it does not automatically refund an already completed payment.
