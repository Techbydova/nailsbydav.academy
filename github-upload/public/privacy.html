Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NailsByDav collects, uses, stores, and protects information supplied through the website, student account area, contact form, and course enrollment flows.

1. Information collected

We may collect and process personal information including your name, email address, phone number, account details, course selections, student progress, and contact-form message details. We may also receive transaction identifiers and subscription-related data from the payment provider when a course payment or subscription is processed.

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, password data, and course selection.
  • Course records: progress, completion status, subscription status, and access history.
  • Payment records: provider identifiers, payment reference numbers, subscription start/end dates, and transaction status.
  • Support and contact records: name, email, phone, enquiry topic, and message content.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser/device data, cookie/session identifiers, and server logs used for security and troubleshooting.

2. How the website uses this information

We process this information to provide the website, operate the student learning environment, confirm enrollment and access, communicate with customers, process payments, respond to enquiries, maintain security, and improve the service.

Examples include: account registration, login authentication, student access, lesson progress tracking, technical support, payment verification, and customer communication.

3. Account security and passwords

Passwords are not stored in plain text. The current application uses a salted scrypt-based password hash before storing account credentials in the local JSON data store. This reduces the risk of plain-text credential exposure if the underlying data is accessed without authorization.

A session cookie named nd_session is used to maintain authenticated student access. The cookie is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and it is valid for a limited session period.

4. Payment and subscriber data

The current implementation uses a Paystack payment integration for student checkout and subscription process steps. Payment provider details, transaction references, billing status, and subscription dates are stored on the site for access and verification purposes. The website does not keep full card data on-site for the checkout flow; card details are handled by the payment provider.

We may also share the information required to complete a payment, verify a subscription, or resolve a failed transaction with the payment provider and any processor that the active payment configuration requires.

5. Third parties and data sharing

We may share personal data with the following categories of service providers where required to operate the website and process transactions:

  • Payment providers, such as Paystack, where the payment and subscription flow is managed.
  • Hosting and technical service providers used to keep the website online.
  • Email or communication providers used for support and account follow-up, if configured.
  • Security or troubleshooting providers needed to protect the website or resolve operational issues.

We do not claim that no third-party sharing occurs. Where personal data is shared, it is only to the extent required for the actual service providers and business operations used by the website.

6. Contact form and support messages

Messages sent through the contact form are saved locally in the site data store and may be reviewed for customer support or service administration. This data is not visible publicly and is retained for operational use.

7. Cookies, sessions, and technical logs

The website may store session cookies and use technical logging to support login, account access, and debugging. This information may include request metadata or operational logs needed to maintain the site.

8. Data retention

Student account, course progress, and contact records are stored in the site's data files. The current build does not include a fully automated deletion process for all records. Access to or deletion of personal information can be requested by contacting support, and the website team will review those requests in line with the current operational setup and applicable law.

9. Security safeguards

NailsByDav uses reasonable technical and operational safeguards, including hashed passwords, session management, and limited access to the account and course data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security against all risks.

10. Customer rights and requests

Customers may contact NailsByDav to ask about account information, subscription status, a request to update their details, or a data access or correction request. Please email nailsbydav@gmail.com or use the contact page for privacy-related requests.

Depending on local law and the current operational setup, you may be entitled to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or to object to certain uses of your information. These requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

11. International transfers

Some processing functions may involve service providers or infrastructure located outside South Africa. If this occurs, data may be transferred internationally subject to the safeguards and contractual arrangements required by the applicable provider and law.

12. Children and minors

The website is intended for general educational use and should not be used by children without the involvement of a parent or guardian where required by law. We do not knowingly market the service to children in a way that requires special additional consent, but we encourage guardians to supervise minors using the site.

13. Changes to the policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as services or legal obligations change. Material changes will be published on this page and the date will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the website after the update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

14. Contact

If you have any privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact NailsByDav via nailsbydav@gmail.com, by phone on 061 785 0227, or through the website's contact page. This policy was last updated on 13 October 2026.

Privacy snapshot

  • Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text.

  • Data is shared only with actual service providers used by the website.
