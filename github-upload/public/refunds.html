Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to digital nail-education memberships and course subscriptions sold through the NailsByDav Academy website. It should be read alongside the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This policy applies to digital learning subscriptions, course enrollment purchases, and related subscription charges made through the active payment flow used by NailsByDav, including limited online account access and course progression. This policy does not automatically apply to unrelated salon appointment bookings or non-digital services, unless those are specifically identified in writing.

2. How to request a refund

To request a refund, contact NailsByDav support by email at nailsbydav@gmail.com, by phone at 061 785 0227, or through the Contact page. Include your full name, the email address used for enrollment, the course or subscription title, and any payment reference or transaction details you have available.

3. Information we review

We review refund requests by looking at the payment reference, the date of purchase, the subscription or account status, and whether the product was used or accessed. We may also ask for additional details if the transaction cannot be matched to an account or payment record.

4. Review and communication

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and will tell you whether your request is approved, declined, or requires more information. We do not currently provide a self-service refund tool or dashboard in the student area.

5. Cancellation vs. refund

Cancelling a subscription is different from requesting a refund. A cancellation request may stop future renewals for the active plan, but it does not automatically refund a payment already made. The result depends on the active billing arrangement, the date of cancellation, the payment status, and the applicable legal obligations.

Access to the digital course content generally continues for the current active period and ends when the subscription expires or is no longer active. The exact outcome depends on the account status and provider configuration at the time of review.

6. Duplicate, accidental, failed, or unauthorized charges

If you believe a charge was duplicated, accidental, unauthorized, or failed after you attempted to complete a purchase, contact support immediately. We will review the transaction reference and the account status before taking any action. If the charge was made in error or without valid authorization, the matter will be reviewed according to the payment provider's records and the applicable law.

7. Legitimate conditions that may affect eligibility

Refund eligibility may be affected by whether the service was used, whether the access or subscription has already begun, whether the account has already been activated, whether the payment provider's records support reversal, and whether other legal or commercial conditions apply. Digital education subscriptions can be time-sensitive and are not always refundable simply because a customer changes their mind after access has already begun.

8. Timeframes

Where a refund is approved, the processing timeframe depends on the payment provider, the payment method used, and the settlement process. We will communicate the expected outcome as soon as the review is complete. This project does not currently publish a separate automatic refund guarantee or a fixed refund deadline that would conflict with consumer rights or provider rules.

9. Mandatory rights

This policy is intended to be fair and reasonable. It may be subject to mandatory consumer rights under South African law, including protections that may apply to digital services or recurring payments. Where applicable law provides stronger rights for consumers, those rights apply to the extent they cannot be excluded.

10. Relationship to the terms and support

These refund terms should be read together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If there is any conflict, the legal rights available under the applicable law and the relevant provider or payment terms will prevail to the extent required by law.
